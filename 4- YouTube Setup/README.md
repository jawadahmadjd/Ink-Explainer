## 📁 Standard Channel Folder Structure

Every channel folder adheres to the standard 3-tier subfolder layout:

```text
4- YouTube Setup/
├── <niche_name>/
│   └── <Channel Name>/
│       ├── YOUTUBE_CHANNEL_SETUP_KIT.md   <- Master channel configuration & copy-paste SEO
│       ├── Branding/                      <- Banner (2560x1440), PFPs (800x800), Watermark (150x150)
│       ├── Thumbnails/                    <- 1280x720 CTR-optimized thumbnail templates & exports
│       └── Metadata/                      <- Per-video SEO titles, descriptions, tags, and timestamps
```

---

## 🛠️ Operating Rules for Channel Assets

1. **Branding Specifications**:
   - **Banner**: 2560 × 1440 px PNG. Central safe area of 1235 × 338 px guaranteed readable across mobile screens.
   - **Profile Picture (PFP)**: 800 × 800 px circular safe crop.
   - **Watermark**: 150 × 150 px transparent/crisp PNG for bottom-right corner.
2. **Thumbnails**:
   - 1280 × 720 px (16:9 ratio, under 2MB).
   - High visual contrast: pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), 3px solid ink strokes, max 3–5 words of bold hand-drawn punchy text, high emotional stick-figure expression.
3. **Metadata**:
   - Save every published video's exact title, description, chapter timestamps, and tags in `Metadata/VIDEO_METADATA_<SLUG>.md` for permanent archival and A/B test tracking.
