# 🎙️ Voiceover Script: "The $5 Daily Habit" (30-Second Explainer)

**Target Duration:** 28–30 seconds  
**Word Count:** 77 words  
**Narrator Tone:** Fast-paced, punchy, engaging, confident (Signature *Infographics Show* cadence ~155 WPM).

---

### Clean Voiceover Text (Copy & Paste for TTS / ElevenLabs)

> What happens if you take five dollars every single day and simply never spend it?
> 
> Tuck it under your mattress, and in thirty years, you'll have fifty-four thousand dollars.
> 
> Sounds decent, right? Wrong. Inflation quietly eats half of that buying power.
> 
> But put that exact same five dollars a day into the S&P 500, and compound interest turns it into over three hundred and thirty thousand dollars. 
> 
> Start today.
