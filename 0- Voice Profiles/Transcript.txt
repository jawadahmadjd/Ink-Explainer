Right now, your existence is sliced into little colored boxes on a glowing screen. Before your feet hit the floor You look at a glass rectangle to see who owns your morning, who owns your afternoon, and how many minutes you get for lunch. You sprint through honking traffic, cram onto a packed subway train, and trade ten hours of your life answering messages from strangers. And tonight, before you sleep.
